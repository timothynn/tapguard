What the gate checks, in order

  1. Signed deny-list — is this card revoked? Works with no network.
  2. HMAC-SHA256 signature — is the media genuine? Verified on the gate itself.
  3. Counter monotonicity — is this a replayed tap token?
  4. Impossible travel — is this card also at another station, faster than physics allows?
token = HMAC-SHA256( key_card , cardID | counter | stationID | timestamp )

The gap, and how it closes

NetworkedGates share a last-seen table, so a clone at a second station is refused in real time.
OfflineEach gate is an island and legitimately accepts. This is the moment a clone pays off.
ReconciledBuffers merge, the collision is arithmetic, the card is revoked and the signed deny-list is pushed everywhere.

The rider channel, on a feature phone

*384#Balance, top-up, last trips and a plain-language reason for any refusal — no smartphone required.
Self-service blockA rider who loses a card blocks it themselves. The deny-list is re-signed and pushed to every gate.
Honest when offlineWith the link down the block is queued, not claimed as applied, and reaches gates at the next reconcile.
