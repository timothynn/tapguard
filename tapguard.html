    What the gate is actually checking

    Signed tap, offlineEvery tap is an HMAC-SHA256 token over card ID, a rising counter, station and time. Gates verify it with no network. Forged or replayed media is refused at the gate.
    Impossible travelNetworked gates share a last-seen table. One card at two stations faster than a human can travel is a clone — blocked in real time.
    Offline reconcileIn a tunnel or on a matatu, gates run offline and buffer taps. On reconnect they merge, catch cross-gate clones, and push a signed deny-list to every gate.
